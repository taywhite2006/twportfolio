Final Reflection

My final reflection of this course.

Throughout this semester, I made several revisions to my major assignments that helped improve both the clarity and effectiveness of my work. For the Article Translation assignment, I removed the section that focused on fraudsters within the cryptocurrency community and instead shifted the focus toward how people often fake their lifestyles and misrepresent success on social media. While the original section was somewhat related, it felt out of place and did not fully connect to my main idea. By revising this section, I was able to make the project more cohesive and focused on the broader influence of social media, which ultimately strengthened the overall message of the piece. For my Discourse Community project, I expanded on the “outside perspectives” section by incorporating more detailed examples and viewpoints. Originally, this section mostly included opinions from individuals outside of the automotive community, many of which were generally negative or dismissive. After revising, I included perspectives from individuals within the car community who may have differing opinions about certain niches or groups. This allowed me to present a more balanced and realistic understanding of the community, rather than a one-sided interpretation. I also added more detail about how members of the automotive community connect and communicate, which helped strengthen my explanation of it as a discourse community. The project I am most proud of is my Article Translation assignment. Initially, I planned to create a magazine-style project that was primarily text-based, but I realized that this approach might not fully engage the audience. As a result, I changed my direction and redesigned the project into a visually appealing, magazine-like layout. This allowed me to combine written content with strong visual elements, making the information more engaging and easier to understand. I am especially proud of how the design turned out and how well it communicates the main ideas of the project. Overall, this course helped me develop stronger writing, research, and revision skills. I learned how important it is to consider audience when presenting information, as well as how organization and clarity can impact the effectiveness of a piece. Additionally, I gained a better understanding of how multimodal elements, such as visuals and design, can enhance communication. Through revising my work and reflecting on my progress, I feel more confident in my ability to produce clear, well-structured, and purposeful writing.
